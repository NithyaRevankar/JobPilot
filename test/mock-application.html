Senior Frontend Engineer

Engineering · Full-time · Austin, TX (hybrid) · Req #ENG-2214

Northwind Robotics builds fleet-management software for warehouse robots. We are looking for a senior frontend engineer to own the operator console: a real-time dashboard used around the clock by warehouse teams on three continents.

What you'll do

  • Design and ship features for a large TypeScript/React codebase.
  • Own real-time data visualization for hundreds of concurrent robots.
  • Work with robotics and platform teams to define operator workflows.
  • Mentor mid-level engineers and lead design reviews.

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years building production web applications.
  • Deep knowledge of browser performance and accessibility.
  • Experience with WebSocket- or WebRTC-based live UIs is a plus.
